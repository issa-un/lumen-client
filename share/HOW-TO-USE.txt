LUMEN — how to use it
=====================

WHAT YOU NEED
- Minecraft 26.1.2, 26.2 or 26.3, running with Fabric Loader and Fabric API.
  (One file works on all three. Older versions won't work.)
- The official Minecraft Launcher. Lumen borrows its Java, so you don't
  need to install anything else.

GETTING STARTED
1. Start Minecraft and wait for the main menu.
2. Double-click Start.bat.
3. Pick your Minecraft from the list and click Open GUI.
4. In the game, press Numpad 8 to open the menu.
   - Left-click a module to turn it on or off.
   - Right-click a module to see its settings.

IMPORTANT: inject only ONCE each time you start Minecraft. Injecting again
without restarting the game loads a second copy, and the two will fight
each other (double hits, lag). Restart Minecraft first, then inject.

To turn everything off, click Unload in the menu or in the Lumen window.
Your settings are saved automatically.


WHAT EACH MODULE DOES
--------------------

COMBAT
  Spear Dash      Swaps to your spear, lunges, and swaps back, on one key.
  Shield Break    Axe through their shield, then a mace hit while it's down.
                  Reaches as far as the weapon in your hand (4.5 with a spear).
  Hit             Hits whatever you're looking at, but only when the hit will
                  actually count. One setting: Chance.
  Aim Helper      Hold Left Alt (change it, or pick Toggle / Always, in the
                  menu) and your aim glides onto one player, with any item in
                  your hand. Built from recordings of your own aim: it trails
                  them, leaves the last few degrees to you and never snaps.
                  Off on the ground unless Ground too is on.
  Mace Swap       Pulls out your mace for slams from 4.5+ blocks. Smaller
                  drops and normal hits keep your weapon, which hits harder.
  Auto Totem      Keeps a totem in your offhand.
  Auto Weapon     Switches to your best weapon when you hit holding a tool.
  Reach Swap      Uses your spear's longer reach to hit players further away.
  Quick Throw     Throws a pearl or wind charge on one key.
  Pearl Catch     Throws your pearl and sends a wind charge to meet it in the
                  air, so you teleport up. Works on a key or by right-clicking
                  a pearl.
  Chest Swap      Swaps between elytra and chestplate on one key.

UHC
  Web Escape      Gets you out of cobwebs and fire with water.
  Web Trap        Webs the player you're looking at, on one key.
  Wind Clutch     Throws a wind charge under you right before a fall that
                  would leave you near death, so you take no fall damage.
  MLG Water       Places water under you before a hard landing.
  W-Tap           Resets your sprint after every hit (more knockback).
  Jump Reset      Jumps right as knockback hits you (less knockback).
  Auto Gapple     Eats a golden apple when your health gets low.

UTILITY
  Auto Tool       Picks the fastest tool while you mine.
  Auto Sprint     Sprints whenever you walk forward.
  Auto Walk       Walks forward for you.
  Auto Eat        Eats when you get hungry.
  Auto Fish       Reels in when a fish bites and casts again.
  Fullbright      Lights up caves and nights.
  Zoom            Hold a key to zoom in.
  Clear Weather   No rain or thunder on your screen.
  Auto Respawn    Skips the death screen.
  Hit Sound       A ding when your hit lands.
  Middle Click Friend   Middle-click a player to add them as a friend.
                  Friends are never hit or targeted.

HUD
  Speed HUD       A speed ring above your hotbar.
  Radar           Shows nearby players around your crosshair.
  Landing         Shows the fall damage you'll take before you land.
  Combat stats    Your ping, fall, shield timing and shield break results.
  Adapt           Learns how the player you're fighting plays and shows tips
                  in the middle of your screen ("They always slam. Shield
                  every drop." / "RUN. Book it."). Optional wheel that turns
                  every time a player hits you.
  Wheels          A wheel floating above players' heads that turns one notch
                  every time they get hit. Choose who gets one and whether it
                  replaces their helmet. Only you can see it.
  Hide other clients   Hides the module lists other clients draw on screen.


GOOD TO KNOW
- No Fabric API? Everything still works except the HUD tab.
- On 26.2 and 26.3: install Fabric Loader and Fabric API for that version.
  Reach Swap and Wheels only work on 26.1.2 (they switch themselves off).
- Something didn't work? Every mace hit, dash, shield break and pearl catch
  writes a line in .minecraft\logs\spear-injector.log explaining what
  happened.
